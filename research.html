Research

Predicting Trophic Interactions

Understanding who eats whom in ecological communities is fundamental to ecology, yet direct observation of feeding interactions remains impractical for most systems. My research develops machine learning approaches that leverage species traits (including body mass, phylogenetic relatedness, and taxonomic identity) to predict trophic interactions. This work aims to improve our ability to construct and analyze food webs, particularly in communities where empirical interaction data are incomplete.

Effects of Microplastics on Freshwater Fish

Microplastic pollution is increasingly prevalent in freshwater ecosystems, yet its effects on fish behavior and physiology are not fully understood. My research examined how microplastic exposure influenced foraging behavior, swimming performance, and body condition in freshwater fish species. This work contributed to a growing understanding of how emerging contaminants affect aquatic organisms at the individual level.
