Research

AI-Driven Food Web Prediction

Develops machine learning approaches that predict trophic interactions from species traits and evolutionary relationships. Trains gradient-boosted classifiers on consumer-resource interactions from the Global Biotic Interactions database, integrated with phylogenetic embeddings derived from the Open Tree of Life. Aims to make food-web inference tractable for communities where direct observation is impractical.

Pennington, P.C., Rubio, W.B., Olson, A.R., Martinez, N.D., & Dell, A.I. Inferring the structure of complex food webs using machine learning. (in-prep)

Pennington, P.C., Olson, A.R., Martinez, N.D., & Dell, A.I. Phylogenetic signal of trophic interactions across the tree of life. (in-prep)

Body-Mass Database Harmonization

Builds an end-to-end Python pipeline that integrates nearly 100 heterogeneous source databases and published allometric equations into a unified body-mass corpus spanning hundreds of thousands of species. Includes a phylogenetic imputation pipeline for species without direct measurements, supporting downstream macroecological and ecosystem modeling.

Pennington, P.C., Hemphill, J., & Dell, A.I. Inferring body size across the tree of life using machine learning. (in-development)

Large-Scale Ecological Prediction

Applies trained interaction models to study food-web structure across continental and decadal gradients, integrating long-term ecological observation networks with historical disturbance datasets. Focuses on how community assembly and trophic structure shift across space and time.

Pennington, P.C. & Dell, A.I. Food web structure and dynamics using the National Ecological Observatory Network. (in-development)

Pennington, P.C., Olson, A.R., Martinez, N.D., & Dell, A.I. Food web assembly following severe disturbance on an island metacommunity. (in-development)

Effects of Microplastics on Freshwater Fish

Earlier research examined how microplastic exposure influences foraging behavior, swimming performance, and body condition in freshwater fish species, contributing to understanding of how emerging contaminants affect aquatic organisms.

Pennington, P.C., Schindler, M.S., Louison, M.J., & Dell, A.I. Group behavioral responses of juvenile golden shiners Notemigonus crysoleucas to microplastic exposure. (in-development)
